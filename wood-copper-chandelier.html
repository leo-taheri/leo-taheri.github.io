PROJECT 07 · PRODUCT DESIGN · MANUFACTURING DEVELOPMENT

Wood & Copper Chandelier Production Development

A small product-development project combining decorative design, CNC-ready wooden components and a custom tube-bending fixture to help a workshop establish a repeatable production process for a new chandelier style.

01 / PROJECT & CONTEXT

From decorative concept to a repeatable workshop product.

A chandelier workshop wanted to introduce a new product line combining turned wooden bodies with curved copper tube arms. My role was to develop the product geometry and help translate the design into a practical manufacturing workflow suitable for repeated production.

02 / DESIGN & MANUFACTURING

Designing the product together with the process used to make it.

The wooden components were modelled and prepared for CNC wood turning so the workshop could reproduce the body geometry consistently. The copper branches were made from bent tube, which required a repeatable way to form the same curves across multiple parts.

To support this, I designed a dedicated tube-bending fixture that the workshop could fabricate and use during production. The goal was not only to create the chandelier itself, but also to establish a small production method around the design.

03 / MY ROLE

Product design, CNC preparation and production-tool development.

I developed the chandelier geometry, prepared the wooden components for CNC-based manufacturing, designed the tube-bending fixture for the copper arms and considered the sequence of production steps required to move from prototype to repeatable workshop manufacture.
