PROJECT 08 · PRODUCT DESIGN · ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

Adjustable Barcode Scanner Holder

A compact 3D-printed workstation accessory developed for laboratory users who needed hands-free barcode scanning, fast angle adjustment and a clean clamp-mounted installation.

01 / PROJECT & NEED

Turning a frequently handled scanner into a hands-free workstation tool.

Barcode scanners were used continuously at laboratory workstations, but users often had to pick up the scanner for every operation. The goal was to create a compact holder that could attach directly to the desk, keep the scanner ready for use and allow users to scan items without holding the device.

02 / DESIGN

Adjustable, modular and designed for additive manufacturing.

The holder was designed as a fully 3D-printed product using ASA for durability and practical laboratory use. The articulated geometry allowed users to adjust the scanner angle and position quickly, while the integrated clamp fixed the system securely to different work surfaces.

The lower mounting structure remained common across the product family. Only the upper scanner cradle needed to change for different barcode-scanner models, making the design modular and reducing redesign work when a new scanner was introduced.

03 / PRODUCTION APPROACH

Optimized for the production scale where 3D printing makes sense.

The parts were designed for straightforward printing and assembly, making batches of roughly 50–60 units practical without the tooling cost of injection molding. The product was reproduced repeatedly and deployed across laboratory workstations, where it received strong user feedback because of its simplicity and usefulness.

04 / MY ROLE

Product design, mechanical articulation and design for 3D printing.

I developed the mechanical concept, adjustable joints, desk clamp, scanner cradle and modular interface between the common base and model-specific upper holders. I also optimized the parts for reliable additive manufacturing and simple assembly in small production batches.
