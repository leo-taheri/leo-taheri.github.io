ABOUT

Engineering across the boundary between digital design and physical hardware.

I am a mechatronics engineer focused on product development and practical R&D. My work combines CAD, mechanism design, prototyping, manufacturing and electronics to turn early-stage concepts into testable hardware.

I enjoy projects where mechanical, electrical and manufacturing decisions influence each other — and where good engineering means balancing performance, simplicity and buildability.
